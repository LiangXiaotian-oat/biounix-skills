## Core Frameworks

### 1. Two Principles of Clear Writing (Ch 2)
Subjects name characters; verbs name actions. Convert nominalizations to verbs. Choose active voice when audience needs agency.

### 2. Old-to-New Flow (Ch 3-5)
Begin sentences with familiar information; end with new. Maintain consistent topic strings. Repeat key terms deliberately.

### 3. Managing Emphasis (Ch 4)
The end of a sentence is most emphatic. Trim trailing words. Shift less important info left. Keep subjects short.

### 4. Concision (Ch 6)
Compress into fewest words. Do not state what readers infer. Watch for redundant pairs, inflated phrases, meaningless introductions.

### 5. Shape Over Length (Ch 7)
Long sentences are fine with structure. Use coordination for equal ideas, subordination for ranked ideas. Keep subjects short.

### 6. Manuscript Structure (Ch 10)
Write in order: Methods -> Results -> Introduction -> Discussion -> Conclusion -> Title -> Abstract. Titles: specific, full scientific names. Abstracts: structured, max 350 words.

### 7. Publishing Process (Ch 11)
Editor decisions: accept (~5%), reject, modify. Address reviewer comments point by point. Tables: if data fits one sentence, do not use a table. Slides: one idea, 4-second rule.
