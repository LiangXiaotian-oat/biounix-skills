# Chapter 14: Biology and Agriculture Examples

## Core Idea
The textbook uses many biology/agriculture examples relevant to plant science. These examples make the writing principles concrete for researchers in this field.

## Example 1: Ustilaginoidea virens (Page 23-24)
Before: In the formation of false smut balls during infection of Ustilaginoidea virens, the pathogen produces two major types of mycotoxins including ustiloxins and ustilaginoidins that inhibit cell division and impose a health threat to human and animals.

Principle: This sentence seems unclear not because of abstractions but because of technical language. To experts in the field, it is perfectly clear. Clarity depends on audience knowledge.

## Example 2: Transcription Factors (Page 94)
Before: Some transcription factors play crucial roles in response to biotic and abiotic stresses from bacteria, fungi, virus, nematodes, drought, salt, high temperature, and so on.
After: Some transcription factors play crucial roles in response to biotic and abiotic stresses.

Principle: Trim the end. Everything after biotic and abiotic stresses is redundant - those terms already encompass bacteria, fungi, virus, nematodes, drought, salt, etc.

## Example 3: GM Crops (Page 2, 179)
The GM crops paragraph appears as both a pre-curriculum test and a coherence exercise. Key errors include:
- have been with modified (passive, missing agent)
- defend benefits mainly to farmers (wrong verb - provide benefits to)
- enhancement nutritional (broken nominalization - nutritional enhancement)
- tolerance drought and stress (broken - drought and stress tolerance)
- containment genes (wrong word - containing genes)

## Example 4: Rice Blast Resistance (Page 383)
From a Cell paper - model introduction structure:
- Context: Rice feeds half the world population; rice blast causes significant crop loss.
- Transition: Non-race-specific resistance is more effective due to broad spectrum and durability.
- Point: We identified a natural allele of a C2H2-type transcription factor conferring non-race-specific resistance.
- Results: Survey of 3000 sequenced rice genomes shows allele in 10% of rice. Single nucleotide change in bsr-d1 promoter reduces expression, inhibiting H2O2 degradation.
- Significance: Novel allele as strategy for breeding durable resistance.

## Example 5: PAL Genes in Rice (Page 384)
Good writing never uses new terms at the beginning of a sentence except the first one:
The phenylalanine ammonia-lyase (PAL) enzyme catalyzes the conversion of L-phenylalanine to trans-cinnamic acid. This conversion is the first step in phenylpropanoid biosynthesis. The phenylpropanoid pathway produces diverse plant metabolites that play essential roles in structural support and defence. Previous studies have shown that mutation of the PAL genes enhanced disease susceptibility. Here, we investigated the functions of the rice PAL genes using AIP, a strong competitive inhibitor of PAL enzymes.

Principle: Each sentence begins with familiar information (PAL, this conversion, the phenylpropanoid pathway, previous studies) and ends with new information.

## Example 6: Plant-Virus Interaction (Page 411)
A review manuscript submitted to JXB on phytohormonal signaling in plant-virus interaction:
After revision, the introduction flows: virus infection -> phytohormone changes -> cross-talks among pathways -> viruses disrupt homeostasis -> review summary.

Principle: The revision creates a coherent narrative by organizing topic strings from familiar to new.

## Example 7: Due To Usage (Page 340)
Biology examples of correct vs incorrect due to:
- Correct: This might be partly due to the strict regulatory constraints (modifies noun)
- Correct: considerable losses... due to poisonous mycotoxins (modifies noun)
- Incorrect: It failed due to poor planning (should be because of - modifies verb)

## Key Takeaways
1. Technical language is clear to experts - audience determines clarity
2. Trim redundant examples after umbrella terms (biotic and abiotic stresses)
3. GM crops paragraph demonstrates multiple common writing errors
4. Rice blast Cell paper is a model introduction structure
5. PAL genes example shows old-to-new information flow
6. Plant-virus review shows revision for coherent topic strings
7. Due to modifies nouns; because of modifies verbs
