# Chapter 13: Exercises and Tests

## Core Idea
Practice exercises from the course to diagnose and improve writing skills. These are the original tests from the textbook.

## Pre-curriculum Test (Page 2)
Correct the underlined errors in the GM crops paragraph:
- Most genetically modified (GM) crops grown today have been with modified input traits, which defend benefits mainly to farmers.
- The GM oilseed crops offer on the market today improved oil profiles for processing or healthier edible oils.
- The GM crops in development offer a wider array of environmental and consumer benefits such as enhancement nutritional and tolerance drought and stress.
- Other examples a genetically modified include sweet potato, which is with enhanced protein and other nutrients, while golden rice, developed with the International Rice Research Institute (IRRI), has been as discussed a possible cure for Vitamin A deficiency.
- Scientists at the University of York developed a weed (Arabidopsis thaliana) that containment genes from bacteria that can clean up TNT and RDX-explosive contaminants from the soil.

Qualification: If you made TWO or more correct corrections, you are qualified to continue this curriculum.

## Short Test - Clarity (Page 18)
Use the first two principles to correct:
1. Accurately weigh 0.15 g of each sample in a 5 mL centrifuge tube.
2. Procurement of mature seeds of P. vulgaris in Chengdu, Sichuan Province of P.R. China on June 28, 2020.

Answers should convert nominalizations to verbs and make actors into subjects.

## Exercise - Emphasis (Page 105)
Underline each term you do not understand in two passages. Then analyze:
- Where do technical terms typically occur?
- How does that difference affect readability?
- What devices were used to revise the first into the second?

Key insight: Technical terms should go at the END of sentences (stress position), where readers are most ready to receive them.

## Exercise - Modifiers (Page 262)
Fix ambiguous modifiers:
1. Overextending oneself in strenuous physical activity too frequently results in a variety of physical ailments.
2. We failed entirely to understand the complexities of the problem.

Fix: Move modifiers to unambiguous positions.

## Test for the POINT (Page 179)
Describe the main POINTS of the GM crops paragraph and list supporting statements.
This tests coherence - can you identify the issue, discussion, and point sentence?

## Practice - Introduction Structure (Page 383)
Analyze a Cell paper on rice blast resistance:
- Identify which sentences are issue and which are discussion.
- Map the structure: context -> transition -> point -> results -> significance.

## Key Takeaways
1. Pre-curriculum test: spot errors in GM crops paragraph (need 2+ correct)
2. Short test: apply two principles to fix nominalizations
3. Emphasis exercise: place technical terms at sentence end
4. Modifier exercise: fix ambiguous modifier positions
5. POINT test: identify paragraph structure (issue + discussion)
6. Introduction analysis: map real paper structure
